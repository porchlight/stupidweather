Stupid Weather

Add Stupid Weather to your home screen

Install it like an app — no app store needed.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari
  2. Click the 3-dot menu in the bottom toolbar
  3. Tap the Share button.
  4. Choose Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the three-dot menu.
  2. Choose Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Confirm Install.
